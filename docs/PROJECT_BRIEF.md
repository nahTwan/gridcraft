# Project Brief

## Product Statement

GridCraft is a full-stack race-strategy simulator where users manage a
real-world team through Monaco, Belgium, and Brazil, make live pit-wall
decisions, and analyze how those decisions affect race and championship
results.

## Design Principle

Realistic outcomes, understandable decisions.

The simulation may use detailed calculations internally, but the interface
should present familiar choices such as tire compound, pit timing, pace mode,
and responses to changing conditions.

## First-Release Scope

- One completed season's teams and drivers
- Three-race championship
- Control of both team drivers
- Pre-race strategy builder
- Lap-by-lap race simulation
- Optional manual intervention on every lap
- Prompts for major race events
- Post-race strategy analysis
- Persistent guest championship
- Data-informed lap-time or tire-performance prediction

## Out of Scope for the First Release

- Live race streaming
- Full-season career mode
- Multiplayer
- Official team logos or broadcast graphics
- Microservices or large-scale cloud infrastructure
- Full account and permissions system
